Our story

Craft cocktail culture, brought home to Southbury.

The Bevy Co is owned and operated by Stevie Sacco, a longtime veteran of the hospitality industry. She opened the doors in May 2023 with a simple aim: pour drinks of real quality in a room where people can relax and unwind.

Come for a cocktail and a few plates to share, or settle in for dinner. Either way, we hope to make the visit one you remember.
